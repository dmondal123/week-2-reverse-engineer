title: Contractor Expense Scope
source_version: "1.0"
status: current
effective_date: 2026-01-01
---

Contractors do not use the employee reimbursement program. A contractor may bill a business expense only when the signed statement of work explicitly marks that expense as reimbursable; otherwise the contractor bears the cost.
