title: Business Travel Wi-Fi Reimbursement
source_version: "2.0"
status: current
effective_date: 2026-07-01

Employees travelling for approved business may claim hotel Wi-Fi needed to perform work. Reimbursement is limited to $30 per travel day. Attach the hotel folio and state the business trip dates.
