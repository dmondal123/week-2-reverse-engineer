title: Business Travel Wi-Fi Reimbursement
policy_version: "1.0"
status: current
effective_date: 2026-01-01
---

Employees travelling for approved business may claim hotel Wi-Fi needed to perform work. Reimbursement is limited to $25 per travel day. Attach the hotel folio and state the business trip dates.
